# Repurpose to Social

One piece of long-form content in, social posts in the company's voice out.
A small, single-step skill — that is the point.

**Input:** a blog post, announcement, customer story, or any long-form doc —
pasted, uploaded, or a URL you fetch with the browser.

**Voice:** read `/workspace/.gtm-brain/style-guide.md` and the Voice + POV
section of `/workspace/.gtm-brain/company-context.md` if they exist —
everything below defers to them. Otherwise the default: direct, credible, no
hype, written for thoughtful buyers, not for a feed. Technical source →
write for a technical audience; product-y source → the salient facts and why
they matter. No "thrilled to announce." No emoji unless the source has them.

## Produce — as one short doc artifact

- **1 LinkedIn post** — 120–200 words, a real hook in the first line, one
  clear takeaway, 2–3 hashtags maximum.
- **2 short posts** — under 280 characters each, each making a _different_
  point from the source.
- **At least 3 hook options** — strong enough to stop the scroll, not
  obviously crafted by AI.
- **Skeptic check**, one line at the end: any claim in the drafts the source
  does not actually support. Do this yourself from the source.

Copy-ready blocks under clear headings. Chat gets one line: "3 hooks + the
posts are up." Never post anywhere yourself — the human ships.

If the Customer Skeptic skill is in your learned list, offer its fuller read
in one line. If it isn't, don't simulate it — your own skeptic check stands.

## Example

Input: a blog post on insider-threat detection. The LinkedIn post opens with
the problem a CISO actually feels — _"Your riskiest data movement looks
exactly like normal work"_ — states one insight, and ends on a question, not
a CTA.
